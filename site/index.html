Three providers, one list.

Connect GitHub, GitLab and Bitbucket side by side, with as many accounts as you need. Self-hosted GitLab and GitHub Enterprise work too.

  • Everything assigned to you, created by you, or waiting for your review
  • Grouped by project, with a provider badge on every row
  • Status and draft state at a glance

Know the moment it changes.

A desktop notification when a request opens or its status changes. Click it to jump straight to the request.

  • Sync in the background as often as every minute
  • Titles in the list turn bold until you have seen the update
  • Choose which events notify you

Filter, sort, mute the noise.

Narrow the list to one project or one state. Flip the order. Mute the projects you never review and they stay out of the list, the badge count and the notifications.

  • Open every visible request in tabs with one click
  • Hide any control you don't use

Private by design.

There is no server and no account. Your tokens and the cached list live in your browser's local storage, and requests go straight from your browser to your Git provider. No analytics, no tracking.
